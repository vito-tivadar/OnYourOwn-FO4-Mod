# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first release)

- Pip-Boy world map: undiscovered locations, discovered locations and discovered settlements.
- Pip-Boy world and local maps: quest objectives, miscellaneous objectives, quest door markers,
  player position, custom waypoint and power armor, each *Always / World only / Local only / Never*.
- World map opens zoomed out and centered while the player marker is hidden.
- HUD compass: whole compass, quest markers, enemies, undiscovered and discovered locations,
  custom waypoint and N/E/S/W letters.
- Floating quest markers in the world.
- MCM menu with diagnostic logging per area.
- Builds for Fallout 4 1.11.240 (`OnYourOwn-1.0.0.zip`) and old-gen 1.10.163 with F4SE 0.6.23
  (`OnYourOwn-1.0.0-OldGen.zip`).
